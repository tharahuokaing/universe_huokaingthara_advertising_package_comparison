## 🎯 About the Module / អំពីម៉ូឌុល

**`universe_huokaingthara_advertising_package_comparison`** is the dedicated analytical comparison tool for **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA Group** advertising clients. It enables brands, media buyers, and business partners to evaluate exact deliverables, audience impression estimates, video commercial placements, and exclusivity rights across all four sponsorship tiers before booking.

## ✨ Key Features / លក្ខណៈពិសេសចម្បង

* **🔍 Difference Highlighter:** Toggle **"Highlight Key Differences Only"** to visually emphasize tier upgrades while dimming shared baseline features.
* **📊 Head-to-Head Tier Filtering:** Isolate specific tier pairs (*Starter vs. Standard*, *Standard vs. VIP Sponsor*, *VIP Sponsor vs. Enterprise*, or *All 4 Tiers*) for focused decision-making.
* **🔄 Monthly / Annual (-20%) Billing Switch:** Dynamically recalculates monthly rates across all columns when switching to an annual commitment.
* **🧮 Interactive Reach Estimator:** Drag the campaign duration slider (`1–12 Months`) to forecast total cumulative views across Starter, Standard, VIP Sponsor, and Enterprise tiers.
* **🌐 Dual Language Switcher (Khmer / English):** Instant toggle supporting Khmer-only, English-only, or bilingual display modes.
* **🌙 Dark / Light Cyberpunk Theme:** Styled with the signature **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA** holographic scanline aesthetic and adaptive theme switching.
